# Geometry Butt (buttplug.io support for Geometry Dash)

NOTE: This will NOT be submitted to the Geode mod index as it falls under NSFW. Don't ask.

### What is this?

A buttplug.io mod that adds support for Geometry Dash, allowing a vibration at an intensity and duration of your choice. You can set this for certain chances:

* On death
* On level start/restart
* On level completion
* On new best
* Progressively rising with the percentage of the level
* On jump

Why not?

### Can I just use this as a regular controller rumble mod?

Yep! While it is primarily meant for adult items, buttplug.io also features support for controllers like Nintendo Switch Joycons and Xbox controllers.
